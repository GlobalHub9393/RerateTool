What this prototype knows

AutoPay: bank account = $10/phone line; debit = $5/line; credit card = $0; no AutoPay = $0.
Signature/FAN: eligible retired plans (Premium PL, Extra EL, Starter SL) = 25%; Premium 2.0 = 20%; Extra 2.0 = 15%; Value 2.0 = 10%.
Current plans: Premium 2.0, Extra 2.0 and Value 2.0 can be mixed by line. Pricing is stored by total phone-line count and the engine tests combinations automatically.
Legacy safeguard: HBO Max loss is surfaced before a “cheaper” rerate is treated as a win.
Training/exploration only. Verify plan eligibility, Signature/FAN enrollment, benefits, promotional eligibility and the final monthly quote in approved AT&T systems before presenting changes.
